Who we are

Label services for independent artists. When an artist has gone as far as they can on their own, we come in with label-level support: websites, release plans, content, booking and the right players and producers.

01 / The founder

Taylor Madrinich

Founder · Guitar · Production

I've been on both sides of this. I play guitar in Whiskey Peddler and other acts, I produce records, and I've spent 10+ years leading sales, marketing, and operations teams. I studied music business at North Central University in Minneapolis, and I played college baseball. That's where the name comes from: you call the bullpen when the starter has gone as far as he can.

When my brother, who performs as SAYWHEN, joined a touring band, I watched the process fall apart. The label fronted the money for the album and told them to go find a manager. Good luck. Nobody was working together.

Bullpen Records is my answer to that. It's built from the inside, by someone who plays the late-night gigs and knows what artists actually need. We take on the parts of the job that wear artists down, and they keep their music, their masters and their accounts.
