How we get paid

No ownership. Just the job.

A traditional label usually takes ownership of the music in return for its help. We don't. You pay for the work, and you keep your music, your masters and your accounts.

  • Flat project fees (websites, EPKs, releases)
  • Monthly retainers for ongoing updates and content
  • Commission on booking where we represent the act
  • Placement and referral fees within the network
