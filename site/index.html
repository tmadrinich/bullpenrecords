01 / Why it exists

Label support without the label deal

Independent artists do it all: writing, rehearsing, playing out, booking, posting, building the website and putting out records. Eventually something gives. A traditional label offers help, but usually takes ownership of the music in return.

Bullpen Records is the other option. We step in with label-level support for the parts of the job that wear artists down, so they can stay focused on the music.
